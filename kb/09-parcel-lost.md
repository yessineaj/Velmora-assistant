# Parcel lost or not received

First check with your neighbours and look for a safe place where the carrier may have left the parcel. If tracking has not moved for 7 days, or says delivered although you received nothing, contact support. We open an investigation with the carrier, which takes up to 10 working days. Then we send a new parcel or refund you.
