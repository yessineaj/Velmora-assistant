# Velmora Express delivery guide

Velmora Express delivers within 48 hours of dispatch. Express delivery costs 9.90 EUR, and it is free for baskets above 80 EUR. Express orders must be placed before 12:00 on a Monday to Thursday. Express delivery is not offered on Fridays or during weekends. Standard delivery is free from 80 EUR as well.
