# Delivery options and prices

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs 4.90 EUR; it is free from 60 EUR of purchases. Express delivery arrives the next working day for orders placed before 14:00 and costs 7.90 EUR whatever the basket size. Pickup point delivery takes 2 to 4 working days and costs 3.50 EUR.
