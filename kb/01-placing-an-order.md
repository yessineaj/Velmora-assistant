# How to place an order

Add items to your basket, then click Checkout. You can order as a guest or with an account. After payment you receive an order confirmation email within 10 minutes, with an order number that looks like VM-123456. We prepare orders from Monday to Friday. Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day leave our warehouse the same day.
