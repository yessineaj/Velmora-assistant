# Payment methods

You can pay with Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, SEPA bank transfer or a Velmora gift card. If you choose a bank transfer, we keep your order for up to 5 days while we wait for the money; after that the order is cancelled. Velmora never stores your full card number.
