# My payment was declined

A declined payment is usually caused by the card limit or a missing 3-D Secure confirmation from your bank. Check both, then try again. You can retry up to 3 times. After 3 failed attempts the card is blocked on our side for 24 hours. Your basket is kept for 7 days.
